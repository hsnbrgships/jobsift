1. Introduction

Jobsift is an independent software project that develops browser-based tools, software, analytics and related services designed to help users discover, organise, filter and understand employment opportunities and job-market information.

This Privacy Policy explains how Jobsift may collect, access, use, store, disclose and otherwise process information when you use the Jobsift browser extension, websites, applications, support channels, research products, analytics services and other products or services that refer to this Privacy Policy.

In this Privacy Policy:

  • “Jobsift”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to the Jobsift project and the persons responsible for developing and operating it.
  • “Extension” means the Jobsift Chrome browser extension.
  • “Services” means the Extension and any Jobsift websites, applications, accounts, dashboards, subscriptions, support channels, research products, analytics products and related services that refer to this Privacy Policy.
  • “User” and “you” refer to any individual or organisation that accesses or uses the Services.

This Privacy Policy describes both Jobsift’s current practices and the categories of processing that may apply if Jobsift introduces additional Services. A reference to a possible future feature does not mean that the feature is currently active.

2. Current Jobsift Beta

The current Jobsift beta is a Chrome extension designed to help users review LinkedIn Jobs search results by identifying and optionally hiding promoted, reposted and certain high-applicant job postings on the currently loaded results page.

The current beta:

  • Supports LinkedIn Jobs only.
  • Does not require a Jobsift account.
  • Does not use a Jobsift backend server.
  • Does not use an external artificial-intelligence service.
  • Does not upload job titles, company names, job descriptions, scan results or LinkedIn search URLs to Jobsift.
  • Does not currently operate cloud synchronisation, billing, subscriptions, advertising, market-intelligence uploads or account-based services.
  • Opens an external feedback form only when the user deliberately selects the feedback function.

All ordinary job-page scanning, classification, filtering, organisation and display processing performed by the current beta takes place within the user’s browser.

4. Information Jobsift May Process

The information processed depends on the Services and features used.

4.1 Job-listing information

Jobsift may access or process information displayed on supported employment-related pages or obtained from appropriately authorised sources, including:

  • Job titles
  • Company and employer names
  • Job locations
  • Job descriptions
  • Job-posting URLs and identifiers
  • Posting dates and posting age
  • Employment type
  • Workplace arrangement
  • Seniority and experience level
  • Skills, qualifications and requirements
  • Salary and compensation information
  • Benefits and employment conditions
  • Job categories and business functions
  • Promoted or sponsored indicators
  • Reposted or duplicate-listing indicators
  • Publicly displayed application or engagement indicators
  • Publicly displayed employer or recruiter information
  • Other publicly displayed job-listing attributes

Not every category is processed by every Jobsift feature.

4.2 Supported search-context information

Where necessary to provide a Jobsift feature, Jobsift may process:

  • Search keywords
  • Search locations
  • Selected filters
  • Sort preferences
  • Pagination status
  • Search-result identifiers
  • Selected job listings
  • Supported-page navigation
  • Interactions with Jobsift filters and controls
  • Dates and times associated with scans or requests
  • The type of supported page on which Jobsift is used

Jobsift is not designed to monitor browsing activity on unrelated websites.

4.3 Settings and preferences

Jobsift may process or store settings such as:

  • Whether Jobsift is enabled
  • Selected filtering preferences
  • Automatic filtering preferences
  • Display and summary settings
  • Notification settings
  • Language and regional preferences
  • Saved-job or saved-search preferences
  • Synchronisation settings
  • Privacy and consent choices

4.4 Technical and diagnostic information

Depending on the active feature, Jobsift may process:

  • Extension or application version
  • Browser type and version
  • Browser user-agent information
  • Operating-system information
  • General device type
  • Language and locale
  • Dates and timestamps
  • Error and crash information
  • Performance and reliability information
  • Scan status and duration
  • General feature-use information
  • Supported page type
  • Network or request status
  • Security and abuse-prevention signals

Jobsift will seek to avoid including complete job descriptions, search histories or unnecessary personal information in technical logs.

4.5 Information provided voluntarily

Jobsift may process information that a user voluntarily provides when the user:

  • Submits feedback
  • Requests support
  • Contacts Jobsift
  • Joins a waitlist
  • Subscribes to communications
  • Responds to a survey
  • Participates in research
  • Requests a demonstration
  • Reports a job listing
  • Creates an account
  • Purchases a product or service
  • Communicates with Jobsift through another channel

This information may include contact details, message contents, attachments, research responses and any other information the user chooses to provide.

4.6 Account information

If Jobsift introduces accounts, Jobsift may process:

  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Account identifier
  • Authentication-provider information
  • Organisation or team name
  • Account role
  • Subscription status
  • Account preferences
  • Saved jobs, searches and dashboard information
  • Account-related communications

Where authentication is performed by an independent identity provider, Jobsift does not intend to receive the password used with that provider.

4.7 Payment information

If Jobsift introduces paid Services, Jobsift or its payment provider may process:

  • Billing name and address
  • Product or subscription selected
  • Transaction amount and currency
  • Payment status
  • Invoice and tax information
  • Payment-provider customer and transaction identifiers

Jobsift does not intend to directly store complete payment-card information where payments are handled by an independent payment provider.

4.8 Independently obtained research information

Jobsift may obtain information independently of an individual user’s Extension activity from:

  • Publicly available sources
  • Open datasets
  • Licensed or authorised datasets
  • Employers and business customers
  • Research participants
  • Data contributors
  • Commercial and research partners
  • Aggregated or de-identified datasets
  • Separately disclosed online Jobsift Services

6. Browser Processing and Storage

6.1 Local job-page processing

All ordinary job-page scanning, classification, filtering, organisation and display processing performed by the current Jobsift beta takes place within the user’s browser.

During ordinary scanning and filtering, the current beta does not transmit job titles, company names, job descriptions, scan results, selected listings, search keywords or LinkedIn search URLs to Jobsift, a Jobsift backend server, an analytics provider, an artificial-intelligence provider or an advertising platform.

6.2 Chrome local storage

Jobsift uses Chrome local extension storage for runtime information such as:

  • Scan status
  • Current-page summary counts
  • Result-page state
  • Pagination state
  • Manual refresh state
  • Content-script operational state

A locally stored result-page identifier may include LinkedIn search keywords, search location, pagination and selected LinkedIn filter parameters.

6.3 Chrome synchronised storage

Jobsift uses Chrome synchronised extension storage for user preferences such as:

  • Whether Jobsift is enabled
  • Selected filtering options
  • Automatic filtering preferences
  • Display preferences
  • Privacy and consent settings

Where the user has enabled Chrome Sync, Google Chrome may synchronise these preferences between Chrome browsers in which the user is signed in.

Jobsift does not independently receive these synchronised preferences through a Jobsift server.

7. How Jobsift May Use Information

Jobsift may use relevant information to:

7.1 Provide the Services

  • Scan and organise job-search results
  • Identify job-posting attributes
  • Detect promoted, reposted or duplicate listings

  • Apply user-selected preferences
  • Display scan summaries
  • Save settings
  • Coordinate scans following page changes
  • Provide requested support
  • Respond to feedback
  • Operate accounts and subscriptions
  • Provide saved jobs, dashboards and notifications
  • Deliver other Services requested by the user

7.2 Operate, secure and improve Jobsift

  • Diagnose errors
  • Maintain reliability
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Protect users and Jobsift systems
  • Improve extraction and classification accuracy
  • Evaluate false positives and false negatives
  • Test and improve user-facing functionality
  • Conduct quality assurance
  • Maintain service integrity
  • Comply with applicable platform requirements

7.3 Develop research and analytics products

Where Jobsift introduces separately disclosed research or market-intelligence Services, Jobsift may use information obtained from appropriately authorised sources to:

  • Analyse hiring demand
  • Identify employment and recruitment trends
  • Study posting and reposting behaviour
  • Analyse salary and skill trends
  • Compare industries, employers, business functions and locations
  • Develop benchmarks, reports, indices and dashboards
  • Conduct labour-market research
  • Develop commercial analytics and forecasting products
  • Support business, economic and investment research

Jobsift will not use identifiable individual Extension browsing activity or job-search history to create unrelated commercial market-intelligence products.

Chrome extension-accessed information will not be commercialised merely because it has been aggregated, anonymised, de-identified or transformed.

7.4 Artificial-intelligence features

Jobsift may introduce optional artificial-intelligence-assisted features.

Before transmitting extension-accessed information to an external artificial-intelligence provider, Jobsift will:

  • Identify the provider

  • Explain the purpose of the processing
  • Explain relevant retention and model-training practices
  • Provide a prominent in-product notice
  • Obtain affirmative and informed consent where required
  • Transmit only the information reasonably necessary for the requested feature
  • Provide a method to disable the feature or withdraw consent for future transmissions

The current beta does not send job-page information to an external artificial-intelligence service.

  • Communicate product or policy changes

Users may opt out of non-essential promotional communications through the method stated in the communication or by contacting Jobsift.

8. Feedback and Support Services

When a user deliberately selects Send feedback, Jobsift opens a form operated by Tally.

Jobsift automatically provides the form with:

  • The Jobsift extension version
  • Browser user-agent information
  • A coarse description of the current page type
  • A timestamp
  • A diagnostic object containing the same categories of information

Jobsift does not automatically provide Tally with:

  • Job titles
  • Company names
  • Scan results
  • Summary counts
  • Search keywords
  • Search location
  • The complete LinkedIn URL
  • Selected job identifiers
  • Screenshots

The user may separately provide information through the Tally form, including feedback text or contact details requested by the form.

Tally processes submitted information according to its own terms and privacy practices.

Jobsift does not use, transfer, sell or license information obtained through Extension permissions for:

  • Unrelated profiling

  • Lending

  • Unrelated surveillance
